SCREENING

No symptoms?

A screening mammogram looks for changes before symptoms appear. The hospital team will confirm if this walk-in service is right for you.

DIAGNOSTIC MAMMOGRAM

Noticed a change?

Diagnostic mammograms are offered during the walk-in week. Tell the team about a lump, nipple discharge, skin or nipple change, or other focused concern. A clinician will assess you and decide whether diagnostic imaging or another test is appropriate.

FOR STAFF

Help visitors find the right path.

Both screening and diagnostic mammograms are available as walk-ins. Ask about symptoms at arrival; direct visitors with a breast change to a clinician for assessment and the appropriate diagnostic pathway. Encourage staff to wear pink throughout the week.
